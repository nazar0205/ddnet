Blackout Legit Avoid source port

The classic planner and isolated simulation design are adapted from OpenKRX (eqsit/openkrx),
distributed under the permissive Teeworlds/DDNet license in upstream license.txt.
This port is modified for DDNet. Preserve this notice and the upstream license.

This is an uncompiled source port. KRX-only death/teleport prediction extensions are disabled;
the planner uses DDNet current predicted world and isolated collision/world copies.
